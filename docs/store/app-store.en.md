## Description (4,000 characters max)

```text
Thuluth helps your family eat well, the sunnah way: a third for food, a third for drink and a third for breath.

Plan the week for everyone at home, from grandparents to toddlers, with halal recipes your family already loves. Thuluth builds a plan around each person's age, allergies, preferences and budget, then turns it into a grocery list with prices in rupees.

PLAN MEALS FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
- Weekly plans for every member of the household, with portions by age
- Desi and everyday halal recipes, with Urdu and English names
- Swap any meal in one tap, or log that everyone ate
- Works offline: today's plan, recipes and your lists stay on your phone

SHOP AND STAY ON BUDGET
- Grocery lists made from your plan, grouped by aisle
- Prices in PKR and a simple monthly food budget
- Tick items off in the shop, even without signal

RAMADAN AND SUNNAH
- Suhoor and iftar planning for the whole family during Ramadan
- Track Ramadan and voluntary fasts
- Learn about sunnah foods, with sources shown and reviewed by named scholars

DRINK ENOUGH WATER
- Gentle hydration goals for adults and children, in cups or millilitres
- Reminders you control, with quiet hours

ASK QUESTIONS, SEE THE SOURCES
- Ask the nutrition assistant in English or Urdu, by text or voice
- Answers show where the information comes from
- Photograph a meal to log it and see what is on the plate

FOR PARENTS
- Follow your child's growth on WHO growth charts
- Picky eating support: the Division of Responsibility, small steps with new foods, and a food log
- Autism-friendly tools: safe foods, first-then cards, gentle food exploration and a Sensory-calm display mode
- No calorie or weight targets are ever shown for anyone under 18

SHARE WITH YOUR HOUSEHOLD
- Invite your spouse, a parent or a helper to see and update the plan
- Export plans, grocery lists and growth reports as PDF

YOUR DATA
- Your family's data is never sold or used for advertising
- No third-party advertising or tracking SDKs
- Delete your account and data from inside the app at any time

THULUTH PREMIUM
Thuluth is free to use for one household. Premium adds unlimited and multi-week plans, more assistant messages, voice and photo, full growth charts, the full Ramadan planner, exposure ladders and food chaining, budget tools and PDF exports. Premium is available as a monthly or annual subscription and renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Thuluth is for learning and planning. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to your doctor or a registered dietitian about medical conditions, pregnancy, or your child's growth.

Terms: https://thuluth.app/legal/terms
Privacy: https://thuluth.app/legal/privacy
```
